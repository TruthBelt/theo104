Introduction to Theology Survey

Each module page attempts to gather slides, readings, self-check questions, and assignment help in one place.

Work through each module in order: watch the slides, read through the Going Deeper section(s), and then try Check Yourself before you take the quiz. The quizzes gauge whether you understand the ideas, not just where they appear in the reading.

Modules

The eight modules build on one another. The course begins with how God makes Himself known (Module 1) and who He is (Module 2). It turns next to the Son and the Spirit (Modules 3 and 4), then to human beings and what sin has done to them (Module 5). It ends with the salvation God provides (Module 6), the church (Module 7), and the things still to come (Module 8).
